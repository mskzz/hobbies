THE BASICS

What is football?

Football is a team sport where two teams try to score by moving the ball into the opposing team’s goal.

Players mainly use their feet to control, pass and shoot the ball. Success also depends on communication, teamwork and understanding each player’s role.

You do not need to be highly skilled to begin. Learning the basics and practising regularly will help you improve over time.

Quick facts

  • Teams: Two opposing teams
  • Objective: Score more goals
  • Standard team: 11 players
  • Casual games: Can use fewer players

UNDERSTAND THE GAME

Five basic football rules

Open each section when you need it. This keeps the information organised and prevents the page from becoming overwhelming.

A goal is scored when the whole ball crosses the goal line between the goalposts and below the crossbar.

Outfield players cannot deliberately control the ball using their hands or arms. The goalkeeper can use their hands inside their own penalty area.

Deliberately tripping, pushing or kicking another player may result in a foul and a free kick for the opposing team.

When the ball completely crosses a boundary line, play restarts with a throw-in, goal kick or corner kick depending on where it left the field and which team touched it last.

In simple terms, an attacking player cannot wait too close to the opponent’s goal behind most defenders and then become involved when a teammate passes the ball forward.

FIND YOUR ROLE

Football positions

Compare the responsibilities and useful qualities of each main position.

Comparison of the four main football positions
Position Main responsibility Useful qualities
Goalkeeper Protects the goal and stops shots. Reactions, communication and confidence
Defender Stops attacks and protects the defensive area. Strength, positioning and tackling
Midfielder Connects the defence and attack. Passing, stamina and awareness
Forward Creates chances and attempts to score. Shooting, movement and speed

PREPARE TO PLAY

Essential equipment

Suitable equipment helps beginners play safely and comfortably.

01

Football Boots

Suitable footwear provides grip and helps players move safely on the playing surface.

02

Shin Guards

Shin guards help protect the lower legs from accidental contact and tackles.

03

Sports Clothing

Comfortable clothing allows players to move freely during training and matches.

04

Water Bottle

Bringing water helps players stay hydrated during physical activity.

BEFORE YOU PLAY

First-session checklist

This checklist helps beginners prepare without forgetting important items or safety steps.

  1. Wear comfortable sports clothing.
  2. Bring suitable shoes and shin guards.
  3. Carry enough water to stay hydrated.
  4. Warm up before intense physical activity.
  5. Listen to instructions and respect other players.
